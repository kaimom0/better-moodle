Better Moodle

A browser extension that puts every Waseda Moodle deadline in one list, with a cleaner layout and grade estimates. An independent student project, not affiliated with Waseda University or Moodle.

Want Better Moodle for your school's Moodle? Email kdp.developer.team@gmail.com with your school's Moodle address (never your login).
